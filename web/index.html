Document library

Every document the assistant can cite, with its issue date. Older circulars that have been replaced stay listed for reference, but answers use the circular that replaced them.

How it works

A general chatbot may state a rule with no source, or repeat one that has since changed. This assistant searches a collection of official documents, puts newer circulars ahead of the ones they replace, and only answers from what it found.

CollectFAQs, circulars, forms and fee notices from UIDAI, MHA, MEA and the OCI portal, with issue dates.
Split by structureTables and step-by-step procedures are kept whole so lists are never cut in half.
Search two waysKeyword search (BM25) and meaning-based search (BGE embeddings), merged with rank fusion.
Rerank for recencyA cross-encoder rescores results; replaced circulars are removed and newer ones on the same topic are ranked higher.
Answer with citationsEach sentence is tied to its source. If nothing relevant is found, it says so.
