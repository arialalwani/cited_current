# Locking and Unlocking Aadhaar Biometrics

## Steps to lock biometrics
1. Log in to the myAadhaar portal with OTP.
2. Select Lock or Unlock Biometrics.
3. Tick the consent box and select Lock Biometric.
4. Once locked, fingerprint, iris and face authentication cannot be used until the biometrics are unlocked.

## Temporary unlock
Biometrics can be unlocked temporarily for authentication. They are locked again automatically after 10 minutes.
