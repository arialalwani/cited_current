# Fee Schedule for OCI Services

## OCI service fees
| Service | Fee if applying abroad | Fee if applying in India |
|---|---|---|
| New OCI registration | USD 275 | INR 15,000 |
| Conversion of PIO card to OCI card | USD 100 | INR 8,000 |
| Re-issue of OCI card on new passport | USD 25 | INR 1,400 |
| Re-issue of lost or damaged OCI card | USD 100 | INR 8,000 |
| Change of address or occupation | Free | Free |
| Upload of new passport and photograph | Free | Free |
