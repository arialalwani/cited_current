# Procedure to Update New Passport Details on an OCI Registration

## Steps to upload a new passport to the OCI portal
1. Visit the OCI Services portal and select OCI Miscellaneous Services.
2. Enter the OCI card number and the old passport number.
3. Fill in the details of the new passport.
4. Upload a scanned copy of the new passport and a recent photograph meeting the photo specifications.
5. Submit the request; no fee is charged.
6. The updated details are sent by email, and no new OCI card is printed.

## Documents needed for the passport update
| Document | Format |
|---|---|
| Copy of new passport (bio-data page) | PDF, up to 1 MB |
| Copy of old passport | PDF, up to 1 MB |
| Recent photograph | JPEG, square, 200x200 to 1500x1500 pixels |
| Copy of existing OCI card | PDF, up to 1 MB |
