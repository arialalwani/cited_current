# Change of Personal Particulars on an OCI Card

## Change of name on an OCI card
A change of name, for example after marriage, requires re-issue of the OCI card. The applicant must submit the new passport showing the changed name, the marriage certificate or a deed poll or gazette notification for the name change, and the existing OCI card.

## Change of nationality
If the cardholder acquires a new nationality, the OCI card must be re-issued with the new passport and proof of the new citizenship.

## Change of address or occupation
A change of address or occupation does not require re-issue of the card. It is updated online through OCI Miscellaneous Services free of charge.
