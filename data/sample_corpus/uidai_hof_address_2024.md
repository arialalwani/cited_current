# Head of Family Based Online Address Update in Aadhaar

## Updating address with consent of the Head of Family
A resident who does not have a proof of address in their own name can update the address online with the consent of the Head of Family (HoF). The resident uploads a document that proves the relationship with the HoF, such as a ration card, marriage certificate or passport. The HoF receives a notification and must approve the request on the myAadhaar portal within 30 days.

## Steps for a Head of Family address update
1. Log in to the myAadhaar portal with OTP.
2. Choose Head of Family based Address Update.
3. Enter the Aadhaar number of the Head of Family.
4. Upload the proof of relationship document and pay the service fee.
5. The Head of Family approves the request within 30 days, after which the address is updated.
