# Aadhaar for Children and Mandatory Biometric Update

## Aadhaar for children below five years
Children below five years receive a Baal Aadhaar. Biometrics are not captured for them; the Aadhaar is linked to the Aadhaar of a parent, and the birth certificate is used as supporting document.

## Mandatory biometric update at ages 5 and 15
A child must update fingerprints, iris and photograph on reaching the age of five, and again on reaching the age of fifteen. This mandatory biometric update is done at an enrolment centre. If it is not done, the Aadhaar may be deactivated.
