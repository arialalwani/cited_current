# Retrieving a Lost Aadhaar and Ordering a PVC Card

## Steps to retrieve a lost Aadhaar number
1. Open the Retrieve EID or UID page on the myAadhaar portal.
2. Enter the full name and the registered mobile number or email.
3. Enter the OTP received on the registered mobile number.
4. The Aadhaar number (UID) or Enrolment ID (EID) is sent to the registered mobile number.
5. Use the retrieved number to download the e-Aadhaar, which is as valid as the physical letter.

## Ordering an Aadhaar PVC card
An Aadhaar PVC card can be ordered online from the myAadhaar portal by paying the fee online. The card is printed and delivered by Speed Post to the address in Aadhaar. A PVC card can be ordered even with a mobile number that is not registered with Aadhaar.
