# Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) Cardholder FAQs

## Who is eligible for an OCI card
A foreign national who was a citizen of India on or after 26 January 1950, or was eligible to become a citizen on that date, or is a child, grandchild or great-grandchild of such a person, is eligible. A minor child whose parents are both Indian citizens, or one of whose parents is an Indian citizen, is also eligible. A foreign spouse of an Indian citizen or of an OCI cardholder is eligible if the marriage is registered and has subsisted for at least two years. Citizens of Pakistan and Bangladesh are not eligible.

## Benefits of an OCI card
An OCI cardholder gets a multiple-entry, lifelong visa for visiting India for any purpose. OCI cardholders are exempt from registering with the Foreigners Regional Registration Officer (FRRO) for any length of stay in India. An OCI cardholder does not have voting rights and cannot hold constitutional posts.
