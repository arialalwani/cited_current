# Re-issue of a Lost or Damaged OCI Card

## Steps to apply for a replacement OCI card
1. Report the loss of the OCI card to the local police and obtain a copy of the police report.
2. Fill in the online application for re-issue of OCI card on the OCI Services portal, choosing the reason Lost or Damaged.
3. Upload the required documents and photograph.
4. Pay the fee for a lost or damaged card.
5. Submit the printed application with documents at the Indian Mission, the outsourced service centre, or the FRRO if in India.

## Documents needed for a lost or damaged OCI card
| Document | Note |
|---|---|
| Police report | Required for a lost card |
| Damaged OCI card | Required for a damaged card |
| Current passport | Original and copy |
| Copy of OCI registration details | If available |
| Recent photograph | As per photo specifications |
