# Updating Mobile Number and Email in Aadhaar

## How to update mobile number or email
The mobile number and email address linked to Aadhaar can be updated only at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra or an enrolment centre. No supporting document is required. The resident is authenticated with biometrics at the centre. An appointment can be booked online on the UIDAI website to reduce waiting time.

## Why the registered mobile number matters
The registered mobile number receives the OTP needed for online services such as address update, e-Aadhaar download and authentication. Residents without a registered mobile number cannot use most online services.
