# FAQs on Updating Demographic Details in Aadhaar

## Which details can be updated online
Address can be updated online through the myAadhaar portal by the resident using the registered mobile number for OTP login. Proof of Identity and Proof of Address documents can also be refreshed online through the document update service. Name, gender, date of birth, mobile number, email and biometrics cannot be updated online and require a visit to an Aadhaar Seva Kendra or an enrolment centre.

## Which details need an Aadhaar Seva Kendra
Updates to name, gender, date of birth, mobile number, email address, photograph, fingerprints and iris must be made in person at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra or an authorised enrolment centre. The resident must carry the original supporting document. Biometric authentication of the resident is done at the centre.

## Limits on updating name, gender and date of birth
Name can be updated at most two times in a lifetime. Gender can be updated only once. Date of birth can be updated only once. Any further update beyond these limits is treated as an exception and must be requested through the UIDAI regional office with valid documents.

## How long does an update take
Most update requests are processed within 30 days of submission. The status can be tracked online with the Update Request Number (URN) printed on the acknowledgement slip.
