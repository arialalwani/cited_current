# Extension of Free Online Document Update in Aadhaar

## Free online document update deadline
In supersession of the earlier notice, the facility to update Proof of Identity and Proof of Address documents in Aadhaar online through the myAadhaar portal is now free of charge until 14 June 2024.

## Who should update documents
Residents whose Aadhaar was issued more than 10 years ago and who have never updated their documents are encouraged to upload current documents online.
