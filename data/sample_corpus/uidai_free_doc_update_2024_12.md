# Extension of Free Online Document Update in Aadhaar

## Free online document update deadline
In supersession of the earlier notice, the facility to update Proof of Identity and Proof of Address documents in Aadhaar online through the myAadhaar portal is free of charge until 14 June 2025. Updates made at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra continue to attract the standard fee.
