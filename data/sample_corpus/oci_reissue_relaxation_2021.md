# Simplification of the Process of Re-issuance of OCI Cards

## When an OCI card must be re-issued after a new passport
In supersession of earlier instructions, re-issue of the OCI card on issue of a new passport is now required only once, when a new passport is issued after the cardholder completes 20 years of age, provided the OCI registration was obtained before the age of 20. No re-issue is required on renewal of passport after the age of 50.

## Uploading a new passport instead of re-issue
Every time a new passport is issued, the OCI cardholder must upload a copy of the new passport and a latest photograph on the OCI portal. The upload is free of charge. A person registered as an OCI cardholder after attaining the age of 20 years does not need a re-issue of the OCI card on getting a new passport; only the upload is required.

## Time limit for the upload
The documents should be uploaded within three months of receipt of the new passport. The cardholder may travel with the old OCI card and the new passport during this period.
