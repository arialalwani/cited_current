# Re-issuance of OCI Card on Renewal of Passport

## When an OCI card must be re-issued after a new passport
An OCI card must be re-issued each time a new passport is issued to the cardholder up to the age of 20 years. The card must be re-issued once again when a new passport is issued after the cardholder completes 50 years of age. Between the ages of 20 and 50, re-issue is not required on renewal of passport.

## Carrying the old passport while travelling
Until the OCI card is re-issued, the cardholder must carry the old passport mentioned in the OCI card along with the new passport while travelling to India.
