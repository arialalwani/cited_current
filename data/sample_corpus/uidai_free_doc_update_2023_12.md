# Free Online Document Update in Aadhaar

## Free online document update deadline
The facility to update Proof of Identity and Proof of Address documents in Aadhaar online through the myAadhaar portal is free of charge until 14 March 2024. After this date the standard fee will apply.

## Who should update documents
Residents whose Aadhaar was issued more than 10 years ago and who have never updated their documents are encouraged to upload current Proof of Identity and Proof of Address documents.
