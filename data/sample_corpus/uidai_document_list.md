# List of Supporting Documents for Aadhaar Enrolment and Update

## Proof of Identity documents
| Document | Accepted for | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Passport | Proof of Identity | Valid Indian passport |
| PAN card | Proof of Identity | Including e-PAN |
| Voter ID card | Proof of Identity | Issued by Election Commission |
| Driving licence | Proof of Identity | Valid licence |
| Government photo ID card | Proof of Identity | Issued by Central or State Government or PSU |

## Proof of Address documents
| Document | Accepted for | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Passport | Proof of Address | Valid Indian passport |
| Bank passbook or statement | Proof of Address | Signed and stamped, not older than 3 months |
| Electricity bill | Proof of Address | Not older than 3 months |
| Water bill | Proof of Address | Not older than 3 months |
| Registered rent agreement | Proof of Address | Valid registered agreement |
| Voter ID card | Proof of Address | Issued by Election Commission |

## Proof of Date of Birth documents
| Document | Accepted for | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Birth certificate | Proof of Date of Birth | Issued by Registrar of Births and Deaths |
| Passport | Proof of Date of Birth | Valid Indian passport |
| Class 10 marksheet | Proof of Date of Birth | Issued by a recognised board |
| Pension payment order | Proof of Date of Birth | Central or State Government |
