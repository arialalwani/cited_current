# Revised Schedule of Charges for Aadhaar Services

## Fees for Aadhaar update services
| Service | Fee (INR, inclusive of GST) |
|---|---|
| Aadhaar enrolment | Free |
| Mandatory biometric update at ages 5 to 7 and 15 to 17 | Free |
| Biometric update (with or without demographic update) | 125 |
| Demographic update (name, address, date of birth, gender, mobile, email) | 75 |
| Document update at an enrolment centre | 75 |
| Aadhaar PVC card | 50 |
